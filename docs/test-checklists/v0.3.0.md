# Please try this: v0.3.0 (Sprint 2, Rides)

On your phone (installed app) and on your laptop, once the preview is live. Locally: `pnpm local:setup`,
then `pnpm dev` and sign in with the local login it prints.

1. **Ride list.** Rides shows all your rides newest first, by week, with rides, km and moving time per
   week. Switch to Month: the same rides by month.
2. **Hide commutes.** Tick it: commutes disappear and the totals follow. Untick it: they come back.
3. **Desktop table.** Click every column header twice: it sorts both ways. Sorting by anything but date
   shows a flat list with a count and totals line.
4. **Desktop filters.** Search a name, pick a type, pick a period, tick Hide commutes, then Clear filters.
   Open a ride and go back: the filters and sort are still there.
5. **Ride detail.** Open a ride: name, date, type, all metrics, the route shape and the Strava link.
   Missing power and heart rate show "—".
6. **Delete and undo.** Delete a ride, confirm: it is gone at once. Tap Undo in the card: it is back.
   Delete one again and wait; import your export again: the summary says "Deleted earlier 1".
7. **Export.** Export → Download CSV: opens in a spreadsheet, one row per ride. Export → Download JSON,
   then import that file: everything is "Unchanged".
8. **Phone and themes.** Check the list and a ride in light and dark; nothing scrolls sideways.

Questions: is the week total line what you want to see? Does the CSV open correctly in your Excel (comma
vs semicolon)? Should Export follow the desktop filters instead of always exporting every ride?
