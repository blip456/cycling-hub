# Please try this: v0.5.0 (Sprint 4, Training plan)

On your phone (installed app) and on your laptop, once the preview is live. The database migrations of this
sprint must have run on the `dev` database first (the Supabase secrets in the `dev` environment).

1. **Profile.** Profile has new sections: Training week (days, hours per week, indoor / outdoor / both),
   Training purposes (rank them with Move up and Move down, at most two), Level (five questions, the
   level is scored and you can override it) and Event (name and date). Save each section, reload, check
   they stay. Try an invalid value and read the error.
2. **Weight goal.** On Weight, choose weight loss as a purpose and set a target weight and date. A date
   that needs more than 0.5 % of your weight per week gets an error and a "Use <date>" button.
3. **Plan intake.** Plan, "Build a plan": the four steps (purposes, level, hours / days / indoor-outdoor,
   start date and event), then the summary with "What the plan will assume". Try an event less than four
   weeks away and more than twelve weeks away and read the notices.
4. **Plan.** Build it. Check the Week tab (today's session on top, sessions only on days you can ride),
   the Overview tab (12 weeks, phases, the current week, the event and taper), and open a session: steps
   with watts and heart rate that match your zones, and the "why this session" line.
5. **Session actions.** Move a session (try a hard day next to another hard day), skip one, mark one as
   done, and press Undo within about eight seconds each time.
6. **Plan updates.** Import a ride file or open the app after riding: a "Plan updated" card appears when
   something changed, with See changes and Undo. Check the changes list (before, now, why) and Undo.
7. **Missed session and matching.** Leave a planned session unridden: it becomes missed and the plan adapts.
   On a ride, "Counts as" lets you pick another session or none; a manual choice stays after the next update.
8. **No power data.** With four or more done sessions without power in 28 days a warning card appears with
   the way to fix it. The plan itself stays the same.
9. **Summaries.** Plan summary for a session, a week and a month: switch weight, goals and the prompt line
   on and off, press Copy and paste it into an AI chat. Read whether the text tells that chat enough.
10. **Calendar.** Download the calendar file and open it in your calendar app. Download again after a change
    and check whether events are updated or duplicated.
11. **Edit the plan.** On Plan or Overview, open Plan options, then Edit plan. The four questions open with
    your current answers filled in. Change the hours, reorder the purposes, add, move or remove the event,
    and pick another level on the summary; the start date is shown but cannot be changed (the text says
    to remove the plan and build a new one). The summary says what changes in plain words and how many
    planned sessions it touches. Press Cancel at any step: nothing is saved. Press Save changes: the
    "Plan updated" card says the plan was edited, with See changes and Undo. Past, done, missed and
    manually matched sessions are as they were. Save again without changing anything: "Nothing changed".
    Try Undo right after a change: the sessions and your answers are back. Try an event after the last
    week of the plan and read the error.
12. **Remove the plan.** Plan options, then Remove plan. The dialog says what is removed (planned
    sessions, done and missed marks, the update log) and what stays (rides, profile, weight log and goal,
    baseline), and that it cannot be undone. "Keep plan" closes it. "Remove plan" removes the plan: you
    see "No training plan yet" and a "Plan removed" card. Check that Summary and Calendar say there is no
    plan, that your rides, profile, weight and baseline are untouched, and that you can build a new plan.
13. Light and dark, phone and desktop: no horizontal scrolling on the phone.

Questions for you at the demo: do the sessions feel right for your level and hours? Is the weight advice
cautious enough? Should an event more than twelve weeks away be an error instead of a warning? Is editing the answers
enough, or do you also want to change the start date of a plan that has started?
