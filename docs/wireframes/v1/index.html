How to review these wireframes

Low-fidelity, grayscale, mobile-first. Scope: Sprint 1 · Foundation & ingestion (sign in, import, ride list, settings, What’s new / What’s next). Charts, zones, FTP, training plan, ride details, nutrition and body composition come later and are not drawn. Every screen has a stable ID (e.g. A3) and numbered pins, so you can write "A3 pin 1: …". How to give feedback: type in the box under any screen, answer the open questions at the end of each section (For product owners / For possible end users; skip what you have no opinion on), then use Send your feedback at the bottom. Your notes stay in this browser, so you can stop and come back later. Sample content is neutral; numbers are illustrative only.

Settings
