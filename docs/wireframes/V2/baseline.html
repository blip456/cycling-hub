How to review these wireframes

Low-fidelity, grayscale, phone and desktop. Scope: Sprint 3 · Baseline, guided FTP test week and zones (part A, wireframes). The athlete profile and the weight screens are in a separate file. Every screen has a stable ID (e.g. Z4a), numbered pins for the phone drawing (1) and D1 pins for the desktop drawing. How to give feedback: type in the box under any screen, answer the open questions at the end of each section (For product owners / For possible end users; skip what you have no opinion on), then use Send your feedback at the bottom. Your notes stay in this browser. Numbers are illustrative only.

Decisions already taken and used here: both test protocols (P39), Coggan 7 power zones and 5 heart rate zones (P40), FTP by hand or as a suggestion from your rides that you confirm (P41), heart rate reference by hand or highest value in the data (P42), you pick the date of each test session (P43). Navigation is the top bar with side menu (P7); status messages are cards at the bottom (P12, P31); metric units (P6).
