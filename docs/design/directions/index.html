Trade-offs

Pick it

  • Soft ink is easy on the eyes; metrics read like gauges.
  • One teal accent shows where to tap first.

Watch out

  • Moody and night-time; teal is common in health apps.
  • Small lightness steps between layers need good screens.

Trade-offs

Pick it

  • Most legible data: vivid metric colours on a colour-free grey base.
  • One clear signal: lime only marks the primary action and what's new.

Watch out

  • Light mode needs two lime tokens: fill #C6F432, text #4D6B00.
  • Busiest of the three; relies on whitespace to stay calm.
